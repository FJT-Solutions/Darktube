## Intent
Shoot "the product taking shape" as a physical event: shards fly back to their places from three-dimensional space, each carrying its own rotation, and the viewer watches a complete image grow out of disorder; after a hold to read the word, everything blasts out of frame, handing attention cleanly to the next shot. Entry and exit are symmetric, so one asset works forward and reversed.

## Core Motion
- 25 tiles each hold a deterministic pseudo-random initial pose: translation ±450/±300/±490px (x/y/z) + ±180° rotation on all three axes, generated by `rand(seed)`, reproducible across renders
- Entry staggers by a Manhattan-distance ripple: `delay = (|r-2|+|c-2|) * 0.045` — the center seats first, corners last, each tile taking 0.34 over `inOutCubic`
- opacity rises fast to 1 via `tin * 2.5` — shards are near-solid from a distance, keeping the "fade-in" feel from diluting the physicality
- Center "REASSEMBLE" text fades in at t=0.42–0.52, out at 0.7–0.78, holding the eye through the hold segment
- The exit does not fade out: each tile flies 620–880px along the direction away from frame center (`atan2` computed + ±0.8 jitter) with ±150° added rotation, accelerated via `inCubic`, leaving frame as solid bodies

## Parameter Table
| Parameter | Typical Value | Tuning Feel |
|------|--------|----------|
| Grid | 5×5, 380×230px, perspective 900px | More tiles give a finer ripple but each tile's presence drops; persp <700 exaggerates perspective and breaks the illusion |
| Initial scatter | dx±450 / dy±300 / dz −210~+490 | Spreading wider makes the opening more chaotic, but assembly paths get longer and need a larger delay window to match |
| Ripple spacing | 0.045 per ring (Manhattan distance) | >0.07 turns the ripple into "calling out ring by ring"; <0.03 and everything lands nearly at once, losing the ripple |
| Entry duration | 0.34 per tile (inOutCubic) | Shorter snaps the shape into being; paired with the ripple spacing, keep the last tile seated by ≤0.6 |
| Exit window | 0.70 + delay×0.5 liftoff, 0.18 duration (inCubic) | **The window must close before t≈0.97**: 0.72+delay×0.7/0.26 once left corner tiles unable to finish leaving, stuck at the frame edge for a second (precedent 2026-08-05) |
| Exit distance | 620–880px (away from center + random jitter) | Too little distance leaves tiles lingering at the frame edge; center tiles get a random direction (there's no "away" for them) |
| Tile palette | 5 neighboring hues derived from `ACCENT_HUE=218`, saturation 14–20% | Changing one hue constant recolors the whole field; raising saturation turns the "neutral poster" into a brand-color block wall |

## Known Pitfalls
- The exit uses translation out of frame, not an opacity fade — a fade reads as "vanishing" rather than "flying away" and the physicality is lost; when tuning, keep `opacity = min(1, tin*2.5)` with no exit term attached
- All random values must go through deterministic `rand(seed)` (equivalent to Remotion's `random()`); true randomness jitters between renders
- The hold segment (t≈0.6–0.7) is the only reading window; longer copy must widen the gap between the label's fade-out and the first tile's liftoff, or tiles scatter before the word is finished being read
- When used as a transition, take only the t=0.7–1 scatter segment, half reversed half forward; pasting footage onto each side's tile faces needs separate texture mapping
